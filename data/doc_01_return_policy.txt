Title: Return Policy
ID: doc_01

Customers may return any product within 30 days of purchase for a full refund.
Items must be unused and in original packaging. Electronics are non-refundable
after 7 days if the seal is broken. Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days
to the original payment method. Store credit is available as an alternative.
To initiate a return, customers must contact support@opsco.com with their order ID.
