Title: Product Note — ProSound X200 Headphones
ID: doc_03

The ProSound X200 is our best-selling wireless headphone.
Battery life: 24 hours. Connectivity: Bluetooth 5.2.
Compatible with iOS and Android. Noise cancellation: active.
Known issue: firmware v1.2 causes audio dropout on iOS 17. Fix: update to v1.3.
Warranty: 1 year from date of purchase. Replacement parts available on request.
