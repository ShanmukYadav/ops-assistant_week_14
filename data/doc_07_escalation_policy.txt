Title: Escalation Policy
ID: doc_07

Tier 1 support handles all general inquiries and standard returns.
Tier 2 is escalated when: (1) issue is unresolved after two contacts,
(2) order value exceeds $500, or (3) customer requests manager callback.
Tier 3 (engineering) is escalated for confirmed product defects affecting
multiple customers. All escalations must be logged in the ticketing system
with a reason code. Target response time for Tier 2: 4 business hours.
Target response time for Tier 3: 1 business day.
