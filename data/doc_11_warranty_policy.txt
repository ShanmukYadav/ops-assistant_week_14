Title: Warranty Policy
ID: doc_11

All products carry a minimum warranty of 1 year from date of purchase.
The MechType K80 Keyboard carries an extended warranty of 2 years.
Warranty claims must be submitted within the warranty period with proof
of purchase. Physical damage caused by misuse is not covered.
Replacement units are dispatched within 3 business days of claim approval.
Customers in warranty claim status retain their original order history.
To submit a claim, email warranty@opsco.com with your order ID and a
description of the fault.
