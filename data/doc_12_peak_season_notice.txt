Title: Peak Season Operations Notice — Q4 2024
ID: doc_12

During the period November 1 to December 31 2024, the following changes
apply to standard operations:
- Standard shipping extended to 7-10 business days due to carrier volume.
- Express shipping guaranteed within 3 business days (up from 2).
- Return processing time extended to 10 business days.
- Tier 2 escalation response time extended to 8 business hours.
- Reorder thresholds for all products increased by 50% automatically.
All customers placing orders during this period will receive an automated
email informing them of extended timelines.
